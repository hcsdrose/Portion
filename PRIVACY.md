# Portion Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 11, 2026_

Portion is a Mac app by Christopher Rose that lets you share half of your screen in video calls. This policy explains what Portion does with your information. The short version: **Portion doesn't collect any.**

## What Portion collects

Nothing. Portion has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking. It doesn't connect to the internet.

## What Portion does on your Mac

- **Your screen.** With your permission (macOS's Screen Recording setting), Portion copies the half of your screen you choose into a window called "Portion". You decide whether to share that window in a video call. Portion never records, saves, or sends the picture of your screen. It exists only on your Mac while Portion is sharing.
- **Window titles.** For a short time after you start sharing, Portion checks the titles of windows on your screen. It does this only to notice when Zoom or Google Meet show their "you're sharing" indicator, so it can hide its purple start-up card. Portion doesn't store window titles or send them anywhere.
- **Settings.** Portion remembers your choices in its menu (for example, which display to use and whether to show the outline). These settings stay on your Mac.

## What your video call app does

When you share the Portion window in Zoom, Google Meet, or another app, that app sends the picture to the people in your call. Its own privacy policy covers how it handles what you share.

## Children

Portion doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.
